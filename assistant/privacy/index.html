Privacy notice

What the Clinic Assistant does with what you type, in plain words. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who runs it

The Clinic Assistant is run by Dr. Abhishek Panda, Assistant Professor of Community Medicine, Government Medical College & Hospital, Sundaragada, Odisha, for members of the Digital & AI Fluency Clinic for Healthcare Professionals. Contact: support@dr-abhishekpanda-cm.app.

It helps healthcare professionals use AI and digital tools. It is not for emergencies (call 108 or 112) and it does not give medical advice about individual patients. It is meant for people aged 18 or over.

Never share patient details

Do not type names, phone numbers, addresses, Aadhaar, ABHA or hospital record numbers, or anything else that identifies a patient, in the Clinic Assistant or in any other AI tool. The assistant refuses messages that look like they contain such numbers, but it cannot catch everything.

What happens to your messages

Where you use itWhat leaves your deviceWhat is kept, and for how long
App: checked notesNothing. The notes are searched on your device, even offline.Your questions and answers stay on your device until you clear them in Settings.
App: AI answers (members)Your question, sent to this website's server and to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer.Questions that no note could answer, kept up to 60 days to improve the notes. A scrambled code for your device, kept two days to count the daily limit.
Website chatYour messages in the current chat, sent to this website's server and to Anthropic's Claude.The chat stays in your browser until you close the tab. A scrambled code for your device and network, kept two days to count the daily limit.
Telegram bot (members)Your messages, through Telegram to this website's server and to Anthropic's Claude.Your Telegram account as a scrambled code, so you need not re-enter the access code (up to a year). Your recent messages for 6 hours, for follow-up questions; /new deletes them at once.
WhatsAppYour messages, through WhatsApp (Meta) to AiSensy, the WhatsApp Business provider, whose AI agent answers from the Clinic notes.Conversations are kept in the Clinic's WhatsApp inbox at AiSensy under its settings, where Dr. Panda's team can read them to help you.

Anything you send with Not right (app and website) or /feedback (Telegram) is kept up to 60 days so that wrong answers can be corrected. Daily totals of messages and AI usage are kept without any identity, up to 100 days.

"Scrambled code" means a one-way hash: it lets the system recognise the same device or account again, but it cannot be turned back into your number, IP address or Telegram identity.

Services that process your messages

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and its server.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the AI answers. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, text sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Telegram, for the Telegram bot, under Telegram's own privacy policy.
  • AiSensy and Meta (WhatsApp), for the WhatsApp assistant, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Clear your questions, or remove your access code, in the app's Settings.
  • Close the website chat tab, or tap the new-chat button, to clear that chat.
  • In Telegram, send /new to delete the conversation context, or block the bot.
  • Ask for anything else to be deleted by writing to support@dr-abhishekpanda-cm.app. Because most records are kept only as scrambled codes, please say which channel you used and roughly when.

Law

The Clinic Assistant collects as little personal data as it can, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For questions or complaints about your data, write to support@dr-abhishekpanda-cm.app.
