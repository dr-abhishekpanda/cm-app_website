About

I work where community medicine meets digital health. I help healthcare professionals move from AI literacy, knowing that a tool exists, to AI fluency: knowing what a tool can take over in their own work and what they must still check themselves.

I trained at S.C.B. Medical College, Cuttack (MBBS) and M.K.C.G. Medical College, Berhampur (MD in Community Medicine). I taught at M.K.C.G. and at Shri Jagannath Medical College, Puri, before joining GMCH Sundargarh in January 2024.

Sundargarh is tribal-majority, depends on mining and carries a heavy sickle cell burden. Malaria, filariasis, tuberculosis and maternal and child health fill the daily work. I teach Community Medicine to MBBS students under the CBME curriculum and coordinate the college's work in NTEP, NVBDCP, RMNCAH+N and immunisation. As a resource person and master trainer, I train others in IMNCI, NTEP, NLEP and biomedical waste management. I sit on the Medical Education Unit and the Medical Research Unit, and serve as Nodal Officer for the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM).

On Doctors' Day, 1 July 2026, I began a mission to promote digital literacy among current and future healthcare professionals, with a special emphasis on artificial intelligence. Six weeks later, on 15 August, it became a free online clinic that meets every evening. I am a Gemini Certified Educator and a Microsoft Elevate Educator Expert, and have completed AI in healthcare programmes from NBEMS, IIT Madras, Stanford Online and the University of Calgary.

The #CM-APP tools on this site come from the same place. Each began with a real classroom or clinic problem, such as a statistic students keep misreading or a calculation a colleague needs for a dissertation.

Free · online · every evening since 15 August 2026

Digital & AI Fluency Clinic for Healthcare Professionals

Bring a problem. Get a workflow.

Each evening we take one or two real digital problems that healthcare professionals face and work through them live on Google Meet. We look at the workflow, find the right digital or AI-assisted solution, demonstrate it and practise it. No lectures on what AI is, and no lists of fifty tools.

Literacy is knowing that a tool exists. Fluency is being able to look at your everyday work and ask: can this be done more efficiently?

Most of the problems are old ones. Better use of familiar tools, plus newer capabilities including AI, usually solves them:

  • Designing data collection tools
  • Analysing data
  • Searching and synthesising literature
  • Preparing academic and research documents
  • Creating teaching material
  • Reducing repetitive documentation
  • Using AI appropriately

Less digital burden. Smarter workflows. Better learning. More AI-fluent healthcare professionals.
