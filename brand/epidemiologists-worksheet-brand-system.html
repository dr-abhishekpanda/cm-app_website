00 — Design thesis

00What the system is for

The look answers to the work, not the other way round. You read routine data, teach epidemiology, and ship small tools — so the interface behaves like a clean field worksheet: gridded, legible, quietly precise, with one colour reserved for the thing that matters.

The four rules

  • Ink on graph paper. A faint 34px grid sits under everything, masked so it fades toward the edges. It signals measurement without shouting.
  • Teal is the only voice. #0C5E4E carries links, actions, and emphasis. If two things are teal, they're related.
  • Amber is a signal, not decoration. #BE651F appears rarely — a caution, a highlight, a single call for attention.
  • Everything else stays quiet. Fraunces with restraint, generous space, no gradients, no drop-shadow theatre.

Why not the usual look

  • The substrate is a cool paper (#F4F5F1), not the warm cream that reads as a default AI aesthetic.
  • The accent is a clinical teal + earth amber, not the terracotta-on-cream cliché.
  • The signature is a real epidemic curve — subject matter as ornament, earned rather than borrowed from a stock dashboard.

Semantic mapping

  • Text — ink for headings & primary copy, ink-soft for secondary, ink-faint for captions & metadata.
  • Surfaces — paper is the page; card sits one step up; paper-2 is the pressed / hover state.
  • Action — primary fills buttons and colours links; primary-deep is its hover.
  • Attention — accent + accent-tint only for a genuine signal (caution, "new", one highlight per view).

Contrast & restraint

  • Ink on paper and paper text on primary both clear WCAG AA for body text.
  • Never place ink-faint on card for anything a reader must act on — captions only.
  • The functional row is intentionally muted so it can't out-shout the brand. Approve, or trim it — the two brand colours alone can carry a whole product.

06 — Motion & accessibility

06Motion & accessibility

Motion is used once with intent, then sparingly. The floor is non-negotiable: keyboard-reachable, reduced-motion-aware, and readable at any width.

Motion budget

  • One orchestrated moment — the epi curve drawing on load. Nothing else competes with it.
  • Scroll reveals — a gentle 14px rise + fade as sections enter, staggered, never looping.
  • Micro-interactions — arrows nudge, cards lift 2–3px, buttons depress 1px. 120–180ms, ease.

Quality floor

  • prefers-reduced-motion disables every animation and shows final states.
  • Visible :focus-visible ring in teal on all interactive elements.
  • Full responsive reflow to a single column on phones; tap targets stay ≥ 40px.

Guardrails

Keeping it consistent

✓ Do

  • Let teal be the only interactive colour — links, buttons, active states.
  • Open every section with a ruled mono eyebrow that names the part.
  • Keep the graph grid faint and masked; it should register subconsciously.
  • Set numbers, units, and code in IBM Plex Mono, always.
  • Reserve amber for one real signal per screen.

✕ Don't

  • Add a second brand colour, gradient fills, or heavy drop-shadows.
  • Warm the paper toward cream — it drifts into the generic AI look.
  • Set Fraunces at body sizes or for long paragraphs.
  • Sprinkle amber as decoration; it stops meaning anything.
  • Animate more than the one hero moment plus quiet reveals.
