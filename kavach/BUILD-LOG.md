## 2026-10-11 · session 3 · chunk 11 (clinical audit fixes)
An independent read-only audit of the clinical logic (separate agent, own test scripts) reported 24 findings: 2 high, 10 medium, 12 low. Each was checked against its source before changing code; all are fixed.
- High · late starters: a child starting vaccines after 1 year was shown both DPT boosters as "missed". DPT-1/2/3 now replace any Penta dose that can no longer be given (not started by 1 year, or series not finished by 2 years), 4 weeks apart up to 7 years; DPT booster-1 follows DPT-3 (or Penta-3) by at least 6 months; Penta rows say "DPT is given in its place"; Rota/PCV/fIPV rows say they are not started after 1 year; full immunisation accepts DPT in place of Penta. Sources: NIS table (Immunization Handbook for MOs 2017, Unit 2), NHM Odisha UIP training deck 2024.
- High · ORS tool: one severe sign (lethargic, not able to drink) or a severe + some pair gave "no dehydration, Plan A". Signs now count once per feature at the worse level, and lethargy or inability to drink shows a red "danger sign: refer urgently" banner with no home plan.
- Medium: JE-2 at least 3 months after a JE-1 given after 12 months; Vitamin A dose by age on the day (2 lakh IU from 12 months); anaemia cut-offs and treatment moved to the AMB Abhiyaan guidelines of June 2026 (WHO 2024 cut-offs, 3-month courses, 1 pink / 1 blue / 1 red tablet, 2 red for breastfeeding mothers, IV iron only after the first trimester) in the tool, ANC flags and tests screen; breath counter under 2 months = danger sign, contact immediately; no WHO grade before term-equivalent age; WHZ/SAM limited to 5 years; SNCU/NBSU graduates get their own visit schedule (days 1–42 after discharge) while the mother's stays counted from delivery; child danger-sign lead says "immediately".
- Low: NIS windows decide "due" vs "overdue" (9–12 m, 16–24 m, 5–6 y); albendazole from 12 months (new 12-month dose) and its dose by age on the day; series limits at 2 years; HPV ends at 15; JE how-text SC left upper arm (IM if inactivated), Rota by brand; Odia swelling sign aligned with the national card (face and hands, or whole body); low BP if either value is low; weight-gain check uses 2nd-trimester visits only; PMSMA from 14 weeks; EDD tool and pregnancy form apply ACOG CO 700 scan-vs-LMP limits.
- Also: Odia vaccine-group headers no longer squeeze into one-word lines when the "mark these given" button is long; PNC heading says "(mother)" when no baby is at home; IFA table adds low-birth-weight babies (AMB 2026).
- Checked: node scripts re-running the audit's cases (vaccines 30 checks, dehydration 11, anaemia/ANC 16, growth 4, PNC 5, dating 4 — all pass); Chromium flow 9 at 393×851 in English and Odia covering every fix (late-starter tracker, Vit-A/albendazole rows, ORS danger, anaemia groups, young-infant breath count, preterm growth, SNCU visit saved with `sncu: 1`, EDD rule, danger-sign wording); regression flows 1–8 re-run without app errors (offline service worker included).
